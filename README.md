## Who I am

Third-year robotics student on paper, full-time builder in practice. I run a one-person studio in Chennai with two lanes: **agentic products for the public**, and **professional websites for Tamil Nadu businesses**.

My edge is a workflow, not a stack. I architect the system, spec the edge cases, and let AI agents do the typing. That workflow shipped a quiz app in 48 hours that won **₹1,00,000**.

## Proof of work

- **₹1,00,000 first prize, Agent-a-thon 2026** - 48-hour AI-agent hackathon at CEG ASTRA, won with [Recall](https://github.com/Hexraei/recall-agentathon) as Team Mavericks
- **3 competition wins and multiple finals** as Team Mavericks
- **National finalist, Smart India Hackathon 2025** - with [CryptoHunter](https://github.com/Hexraei/CryptoHunter), a security analytics platform built for a government-sector problem
